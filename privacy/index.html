Orivo Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Orivo is a day-planning app. This page describes, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it.

Your tasks

Orivo stores your tasks on your device and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud account. We do not have a server, we do not run one, and we cannot see your tasks, your notes, or anything else you type into the app. Apple hosts your synced data in your private iCloud database — not ours.

What we don't collect

Orivo does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information. We don't require an account or a sign-in.

Anonymous product analytics

Orivo uses TelemetryDeck to understand, in aggregate, how the app is used — for example, how many people complete a task on a given day. TelemetryDeck does not use cookies, does not use advertising identifiers, and cannot connect an event back to you individually. No task content is ever included in an analytics event — only anonymous counts of app actions (like "a task was completed").

Crash reports

Orivo uses Sentry to receive crash reports so we can fix bugs. A crash report contains a stack trace (which line of code failed) — it does not contain your tasks, notes, or any personal data.

Permissions Orivo may ask for

  • Notifications — to remind you about tasks with an alert set.
  • Calendar / Reminders — only if you turn on sync, to show your existing events and reminders alongside your Orivo tasks. This access is read-only.
  • Camera — only if you use Schedule Scan, to photograph a printed schedule (like a class timetable or shift rota) and turn it into tasks. The photo is processed on your device and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Photos (Add Only) — only if you save an exported schedule image (from Schedule Scan or Share Schedule) to your photo library.

Orivo never asks for microphone, contacts, or location access, and doesn't use any of them.

In-app purchase

Orivo Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We never see your payment details.

Children's privacy

Orivo is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
